# Assistant and panel

← [README](../../README.md)

## Voice chat

The **Assistant** page needs the microphone, and browsers only allow it over https. The panel therefore also listens on `https://SPARK:31443` with a self-signed certificate; the browser warns once. The LLM connection is under Settings → Language model (default: qwen38 at `http://127.0.0.1:30001/v1`). Since V01.0.292 "Model" there offers a choice of the models the server at this address reports (`/v1/models`, admin only, kept 60 seconds, "Reload" asks at once); "Automatic" takes the first, "Type another …" opens the text field. If the list cannot be read, the text field stays with the reason above it. The installer takes the qwen38 API key from `~/.config/qwen38/api-key` of the user who runs `sudo ./install.sh`. Thinking is off for the chat so the answer starts right away. An animated assistant face shows whether it is listening, thinking or speaking; its mouth follows the voice. The same face sits in the bottom right corner of every tab, so you can talk from anywhere in the panel. Since V01.0.154 the admin picks under Settings → Defaults → "Gesicht des Assistenten" between *Robot* (as before, the default) and *Comic* (a caricature with spiky hair and a short mustache, since V01.0.187 only above the upper lip). Since V01.0.497 there is a third choice, *Comic (woman)*: a woman with long dark hair and a side parting, drawn in the same style, with lashes and lips that open when she speaks; she moves like the comic face and shows the expressions too. The iPhone app, the companions (Mac and Windows, as a still) and the Pebble draw her too. The comic face looks around, blinks with heavy lids, raises its brows, tilts its head, moves mouth and mustache with the voice and shows the state with a coloured ring: grey idle, blue listening, yellow thinking, green speaking, red error. The choice applies to everybody, the iPhone app included; more faces can be added in `static/js/face.js`. Since V01.0.521 every profile picks its own face under Me → Conversation → *Display* with pictures (*Default*, *Robot*, *Comic*, *Comic (woman)*); it applies on all of the profile's devices (panel, iPhone app, companions, Pebble), *Default* takes the Spark's face. In the iPhone app the same choice sits at the top of Settings → *Face* (with the next TestFlight build). The admin's pick has since been at the top of Settings → Defaults → *Face*, with pictures as well, and applies to guests, shared devices and everybody who picked nothing of their own; the presets never copy a face into a profile. The server only takes the fixed names (`profiles.FACES`), every device asks through `profiles.face_of`. Since V01.0.241 the small picture before each answer in the history shows the chosen face as well. Since V01.0.294 there is a switch "Gesicht zeigt, was es tut" next to it (off): the face then shows a small sign beside the head for what the answer is doing, taken only from the panel's events, never from the model's text: a magnifier for web, document or history searches, a calendar sheet for calendar and briefing, a letter for mail, a light bulb for Home Assistant, a note pad for memory and reminders, thought bubbles while thinking. After an action was done (device switched, something remembered, reminder set) it is pleased for a moment and winks, on an error it looks puzzled with a question mark, and after 5 minutes without conversation or use it falls asleep with "zzz"; a tap, a key or the mouse over the face wake it.

**Start page and password:** the panel opens with the assistant. Monitoring, settings, voices and logs need the panel password (button "Settings"; the login ends after 7 days without use and renews itself while in use). "Assistant without password" under Settings → Security is off on new installs: then guests are locked out, profiles sign in on the start page with name and PIN (devices with a key keep working), and the admin signs in with "Admin". Switched on, the assistant is open to everyone on the network, also without a profile. **Guests only get questions and answers** (since V01.0.509, Dominik 2026-10-13): the language model answers from its own knowledge, without any tool, so no web search, timers, briefing, memory, history on the Spark or smart home. Above the history it then says "Without a profile: questions and answers only" with a *Sign in* button. The same holds for a voice a shared speaker does not recognize as its owner's, and for the admin in a browser without an own profile. The password can be changed under Settings → Security. Scripts can still use HTTP Basic.

**Release** (since V01.0.509, Dominik 2026-10-13): what the admin switches on under Features works at once for every profile. There is no own switch per profile for it any more. Only the admin blocks single profiles, under People and devices → Blocked functions or in the function's detail. A profile only sets up its part (enter a place, connect an account, pair a device). It can only switch off the few things the Spark does unasked, such as notes on its own, notifications to the phone, the morning sheet or night updates of its speakers; these are on from the start.

**Blocked, but own data (V01.0.531, Dominik 2026-10-14):** when the admin blocks a released function for one profile, its page under Me disappears; what the profile stored for it stays its own. While something is left, Me shows in place of the page only a short card: "The admin blocked "Weather" for you (People and devices → Blocked functions). Stored: 1 entry." and the button "Delete data" (with a question first). It deletes exactly what deleting on the function's own page removes, passwords and keys included (place, stop, calendars, mailboxes, address books, reminders, voice samples, Home Assistant connection, Telegram link, paired devices, MCP connections and services, the reMarkable with its documents, knowledge folders, rules of "Proactive" and of inbox tidying); then the card goes. Not included are all documents at once and chats, because other people belong to them. Technically every module registers its data in `features.OWN` (count and delete), `/api/whoami` names under `locked` only function, page and count, never contents; deleting goes through `DELETE /api/profile/owndata/{key}` (only the profile's own browser login, never a device key or guest, only while blocked, rate limit `owndata`). Journal: "profiles: own data deleted while blocked | weather | 1 entries …" (area memory).

**On the phone:** below 760 px the page opens with the big face (tap = talk) and two buttons below it: "Settings" opens the "Me" window ("Hey Spark" at the top), "History" the messenger view. That view fits the screen exactly: a slim top bar with an arrow back to the face, a small face, state, profile and menu, the history in the middle, and at the bottom a text field and round mic button (a stop button during the answer). The browser remembers which of the two views was open last. The menu (☰) holds new conversation, earlier conversations, conversation settings, language, light/dark and, for admins, every tab.

**Like the app (V01.0.296):** on a phone (below 760 px) the page looks like the iPhone app, the same on iPhone and Android. At the bottom are the tabs *Spark* (conversation), *Today* (setup and the same cards and sentences as Me → Today), *Documents*, *Me* and *Manage*; you only see what your role allows (guests only Spark, Manage only with admin rights, Documents only when allowed). Spark shows the face at the top (smaller once there are bubbles), the history below, the big mic button and always the text field; top left "History" (earlier conversations as a sheet from below, swipe down closes) and "New". Me and Manage are lists like the iOS settings; sub-pages slide in from the right, "‹ Back" top left, the Android or Safari back gesture goes exactly one level back. Manage starts with the status, then Features, People and devices, Settings, Logs, Update, Backups and Appearance. Under Appearance (Manage or Me) "Show the computer layout" switches back to the old phone view (this browser only); the menu's "Show like the app" returns. It is only another presentation: no new endpoints, the server checks rights as before. Questions and answers use the width like the iPhone app: bubbles start about 16 px from the edge and run up to 40 px before the other edge; "Read again" and "Delete" show only after a tap on the message, below the bubble, and never make it narrower.

**Typing comes first (V01.0.701):** while something is typed into the text field, the face draws only about ten pictures a second instead of sixty (until 1.5 s after the last letter), and the page asks which face is on screen only four times a second. Typing stays smooth on slower phones, tablets and in the Android app. In the iPhone app the line to type in is its own view: a letter redraws only that line, not the face and the conversation.

**Typing comes first, part 2 (V01.0.749):** while text stands in the field, the field is marked "hastext" instead of "typing": the old name started an answer's waiting dots (`.typing::after`) invisibly at the field, and they laid out the whole page sixty times a second. The face's loading wheel turns only while it shows (thinking, searching). The conversation is closed off (`#chatlog{contain:strict}`): a letter no longer lays out every bubble of a long conversation again. Measured with the CPU slowed down six times and 120 bubbles: long tasks while typing from about 150 ms to 0, time to the next picture on average from 37 to about 25 ms.

**Quick start and tidier pages (V01.0.750):** an admin's page start on a slow phone (CPU throttled four times) took 11.5 s, now about 2 s; with only a profile 2.1 s, now about 1.8 s. The jump links asked for every hint whether a menu is visible, and the browser recalculated the whole page each time; that now happens once per frame. The cards under Status (Listening now, Services, NAS, Usage and others) ask the Spark only while Status is open, all together when it opens. The list of all guides is drawn under Features → Guides, the text behind „Guide“ at a switch at the first tap. The start page goes out gzipped (212 KB → about 40 KB), and every script and style file carries its fingerprint instead of the version number: after an update the browser loads only what changed and keeps the rest up to a year. On computers the face column is 320 instead of 420 px wide (buttons and switches one below the other), the conversation gets the room. Away from the assistant every page ends with room for the floating face: its last rows can always be scrolled out from under it, the width of the pages stays the same. Status has its menu like Settings: symbols and the blocks Watch (Monitoring, Live, Logs), Check (Checks, Improve, Hands-on test) and Technology (Usage, Models, Architecture); the footer stands at the bottom of the window. On phones „Manage“/„Verwalten“ fits whole into the back button. Since V01.0.758 Features and People and devices have their symbols too: every page in the menu beside them, and every group of Features carries the symbol of the same group under Me. Since V01.0.764 the list of profiles is tidy: a coloured circle with initials, one short line "last … · 2 devices · 30 facts", setup as a thin bar with "14/15", a chevron instead of "Details" (the whole line opens the person), search field and selects of one height, "+ New profile" beside the count; devices show their kind as a symbol (speaker, iPhone app, watch, terminal, Home Assistant, own key).

**As an app on your phone:** open `https://SPARK:31443` in the phone's browser, accept the certificate warning, then use "Add to Home screen" in the browser menu (iPhone: Safari → Share → "Add to Home Screen"). The assistant then starts full screen with its own icon. With the self-signed certificate Android creates a shortcut instead of an installed app; it works the same, it just does not show up in the app list.

**Better recognition:** a fixed default language (Settings → Speech recognition, e.g. German) instead of "auto" helps most with short sentences. The "Context" field takes names and terms that are often misheard; the model then prefers them. The 1.7B model is noticeably more accurate than 0.6B and needs about 2–3 GiB more memory.

**Second recognizer: Parakeet.** Under Settings → Speech recognition → "Recognizer" the admin switches between Qwen3-ASR (GPU) and Parakeet ([primeline/parakeet-primeline](https://huggingface.co/primeline/parakeet-primeline), a German fine-tune of NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3; the download is [flozen1981/parakeet-primeline-onnx](https://huggingface.co/flozen1981/parakeet-primeline-onnx), an int8 ONNX export of it, run via sherpa-onnx; idea from [winidi/dictate](https://github.com/winidi/dictate)). Parakeet runs on the CPU and needs about 1 GiB; the Qwen3-ASR engine is stopped meanwhile. It ignores the context field, and a stream returns the whole text in one piece. The first start downloads about 640 MB.

**Conversation comfort:** the assistant knows the date and time (the browser's time zone; can be turned off under Settings → Language model). Conversations are kept in the browser and can be reopened or deleted at the top of the conversation. With "live transcript" the text appears while you speak; in the pause the recognition is usually already done, so that step is skipped at the end.

**Guides:** every service that can be switched on has an openable guide (what it does, what you need, setup, example phrases, what leaves the house, switching off, troubleshooting): next to its switch under Features, at the top of its page in the "Me" window and collected under Features → Guides. Services are sorted into the same groups everywhere: Conversation, My day, Mail and documents, Home, On the go and devices, Security and access. The texts live in `app/panel/static/js/guides.js`; a self-test checks that no switch is left without a guide and group.

**Weather, contacts, parcels (V01.0.93):** three small services, each off by default; once the admin releases it under Features it works at once for every profile (each profile sets up the place for the weather and the address book in the "Me" window); never guests. *Weather* comes straight from Open-Meteo (no key, only the place's coordinates leave, own address configurable); the Spark builds fixed sentences from the numbers, also for the daily briefing and the proactive weather note. *Contacts* reads an address book over CardDAV (iCloud: https://contacts.icloud.com with an app-specific password, Nextcloud, others) once a day and keeps it encrypted: phone numbers, e-mail addresses, birthdays (in the briefing and as a notice), names instead of addresses in mail. Contact text counts as outside text. *Parcels* recognises shipping mails of DHL, Hermes, DPD, GLS, UPS and Amazon in the profile's own mailbox (14 days) and says what comes today, is on its way, waits for pick-up or was delivered; no carrier is asked, parcel lines count as mail text.

**Telegram (V01.0.96):** your own bot from @BotFather (guide in the panel). The admin switches it on under Features and enters the token (stored encrypted); the Spark asks Telegram for new messages itself, no open port needed. Each profile links its Telegram in the "Me" window with a one-time code (10 minutes) and writes or sends voice messages (speech recognition on the Spark). Strangers get one fixed sentence, groups nothing. Per profile, all off at first: answers also as voice messages, notifications over Telegram, personal data (calendar, mail, contacts, parcels, documents; also appointments and mail in notifications) and switching the smart home, only with the code word. Commands: /neu, /stimme, /trennen. When a message names a place in the panel ("Me → Telegram"), it gets an "Open in the panel" button if the admin entered the panel address for links (https) under Features → Telegram (since V01.0.448).

**Own speakers (ESP32, V01.0.99, without display since V01.0.104):** small ESP32-S3 boards (16 MB flash, PSRAM, INMP441 microphone, MAX98357A amplifier, e.g. the XiaoZhi breadboard boards) become a voice assistant in the room. Firmware: the open XiaoZhi firmware at a fixed version, German, wake word "Jarvis", without a built-in cloud address; a GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/firmware.yml`, settings in `firmware/xiaozhi.json`) builds it as release `fw-<version>`. The Spark fetches the newest one daily (or on a button), checks every file against `manifest.json` and keeps it with the previous one. Set up in the "Me" window → Speakers: over USB from Chrome or Edge (Web Serial, esptool-js 0.5.7 ships with the panel) the browser writes firmware and a settings block (Wi-Fi, Spark address, device key); the Wi-Fi password never reaches the Spark. Without a PC a board shows or says a 6-digit code. Each speaker is a device key of its profile (voice, memory, smart home with code word, room). Updates arrive over Wi-Fi when the board starts (per device "auto" can be switched off) or over USB. Admin switch `chat.esp32`, off at first, released for every profile; the reverse proxy must pass WebSockets. Board variants: no display, OLED 128×32, OLED 128×64, colour display, since V01.0.133 the Waveshare ESP32-S3-AUDIO-Board (round board with ES8311 audio chip and two microphones; display and camera are optional there). When a board is rewritten over USB with another variant, updates follow the board type it reports at start; the no-display variant never touches the display pins and, since firmware 2.5.1.3 (V01.0.132), no longer loads font and emoji either, which made it crash right after joining the Wi-Fi. Start check only by POST of at most 16 KB, at most 3 open codes per address, firmware only from github.com downloads with a size limit.

**Device updates (V01.0.381):** Features → "Device updates" (admin, off by default) makes the Spark look for new firmware for the speakers, the Android app and the Spark companion every 6 hours instead of once a day, as before only from GitHub's download addresses and with size and checksum from the manifest; *Look now* in Status → Device updates asks at once. The card shows per kind of device the version on the Spark, the last check and every device with its version. A profile with such a device gets one notification per new version, and so does the profile for admin notes; the version the Spark already has when this is switched on is not announced. The companion keeps showing its own updates. My status has one row per kind of device ("update comes tonight", "1 of 2 up to date"), the live view the target "Geräte-Updates" with counts. **Update speakers at night** (sub-switch; on from the start for every profile, can be switched off under Me → Conversation → Devices → "Update my speakers at night", plus "Updates by themselves" at the speaker): the boards fetch firmware only when they start. In the night window (default from 05:15 for 60 minutes, after the window of the nightly Spark update) the Spark first restarts one connected, unused speaker. When it comes back with the new version within 10 minutes, the others follow one by one, the next one 30 minutes later at the earliest. When it does not come back, the round stops for this version: owner and admin get a notification, and it goes on only after *Go on distributing* in Status. Never during a conversation, in room mode, during a test, a Notaus or own Notaus, and never during a Spark update. **Note on a new XiaoZhi version** (sub-switch): the Spark compares the newest release of github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32 with the point our firmware builds on and sends the admin one note per new version. Nothing is built by itself; only the release's version number is used, never its text. Since V01.0.678 GitHub's answer may be up to 4 MB: it lists every file of the release (XiaoZhi: one package per board) and 256 KB was too tight ("XiaoZhi check failed: TooLarge" in the error log). **Firmware 2.5.2.8 (V01.0.711):** our speaker firmware now builds on XiaoZhi 2.5.2 (commit 49b4380). Little changes for our boards: the ESP32-S3 sets up microphone and speaker (I2S) the same way everywhere, and an unreadable serial number in the chip is ignored. All own patches (own server only, board without display, wait instead of dropping sound, Notaus button, quiet at once on the wake word, stop while it speaks) apply unchanged. Boards take only newer versions by update; going back to 2.5.1.7 works only over USB.

**Interrupting a speaker (V01.0.472):** while an own speaker talks, the board only listens for its wake word "Jarvis" (with echo cancelling where the board has a reference channel, such as the Waveshare board) or the button. Since firmware 2.5.1.7 it is then quiet at once: the firmware drops the rest of its play queue (`firmware/patch_unterbrechen.py`) instead of playing it for up to 1.2 s, and listens. Saying only "Stopp", "Halt", "Ruhe", "Hör auf" or "Danke, das reicht" then (fixed word list `esp32.STOP_WORDS`, the whole sentence) sends nothing to the language model: the speaker stays quiet and waits for its wake word again; Check shows "Stopp gehört". Any other question is answered as always. **Stop while it speaks** (admin: Features → Own speakers → Stop while it speaks, off at first, then for every profile): then it works without the wake word at all. At every connection the Spark asks the board through the MCP tool `self.spark.stop_word` (`firmware/patch_stoppwort.py`) to keep its microphone on while speaking; only boards with a reference channel do so, their echo cancelling takes out the own voice (breadboard boards keep it off). The Spark cuts what arrives while speaking into short pieces (at most `STOP_PIECE` = 3 s, one recognition at a time) and runs them through the speech recognition; only a whole sentence from `STOP_WORDS` counts, and not when it is in the last four sentences said (a "halt" in its own sentence is echo). It then ends the answer, sends `tts stop` with `spark_quiet`, the board drops its queue and the Spark listens. Nothing of it is stored; the log only says "stop word while speaking", the live overview counts "Lautsprecher hört auf „Stopp““ under connections. When the switch goes off, a board asked before is told "off" once at its next connection.

**Checking a speaker (V01.0.131):** under Me → Speakers every speaker has a *Check* button. It shows whether the admin switch is on and the profile is not blocked, whether there is firmware for the board variant, whether the board ever checked in, which address is in the board (with a warning for `https://<IP>` or port 31443, whose self-signed certificate the board refuses, for `localhost` and for a missing address) and what the speaker did lately: start check, connection, refused connection with its reason, wake word, microphone (length, loudest moment and mean in dB, whether there was speech), what was understood and how much sound went back. These lines stay in memory only (40 per speaker, gone when the panel restarts). *Check network* asks the address from the Spark the way the board does: `GET /api/esp32/ping` (answers with a marker of this Spark) and a WebSocket with `x-spark-probe: 1`; this shows whether the address leads to this Spark, whether the certificate is accepted, whether the reverse proxy redirects or blocks WebSockets. Allowed like Home Assistant (home network yes, the Spark's own service ports no), only these two paths, answers of at most 4 KB, six checks in ten minutes per profile. Behind a router without NAT loopback the check can fail although the board reaches the Spark. *Test: sound and microphone* plays the soft tone and a test sentence at the next "Jarvis" or button press (at once when connected) and then checks the microphone with the next sentence: the speaker says what it understood, or that it heard nothing. *Read the board's log (USB)* connects the board in Chrome or Edge, restarts it and reads for 45 seconds what the firmware reports; the page translates the known lines (Wi-Fi joined or not, certificate refused, Spark unreachable, status 403, pairing code, conversation connection, crash, brownout, sound error). The text stays in the browser, words like token or password are blacked out; *Copy* takes it along for a bug report. Since V01.0.136 the list under *Check* follows live (every 2 seconds while the page is visible); in room mode it only says that a sentence was heard and whether it speaks in a pause, never what was heard. Since V01.0.135 every speaker has a *Volume* slider under Me → Speakers (0 to 100; the Spark sends it at every connection through the firmware's MCP tool `self.audio_speaker.set_volume`, at once when connected) and a *Microphone* choice less sensitive / normal / more sensitive (from which level the Spark takes sound as speech: about −41, −47 or −53 dB). Since V01.0.134 a quiet level from about −47 dB (and three times above the background noise) counts as speech, quiet recordings are raised up to 20 times before speech recognition, and every question writes a line "esp32: microphone at …" with length and level to the log. The log now also shows the start checks ("esp32: start check from …") and the reason of every refused connection. Since V01.0.137 the Spark buffers the sound: it starts only when 0.6 seconds are ready, keeps at most 0.9 seconds in flight (since V01.0.138: 0.7) (the board holds 1.2 seconds) and after a stall collects 0.3 seconds again instead of sending single snippets. When speech output could not keep up, *Check* shows "Ton stockte N-mal" and the log "esp32: sound stalled"; if it stutters without that line, the board's Wi-Fi is the cause. Since V01.0.138 (firmware 2.5.1.5) the board no longer drops sound when its buffer is full but waits; the connection then slows the Spark down. Before, Wi-Fi delays added up in long answers, the buffer overflowed and the gaps grew towards the end. The Spark's lead is lowered to 0.7 seconds so old firmware has more room too. The board fetches the new firmware itself at its next start (unplug it briefly).

**Speaker page (V01.0.149):** Me → Speakers shows "My speakers" at the top (one line per speaker with its state: connected, room mode until, update available) and "Add a speaker" with two ways: *Set up a new board over USB* (Chrome/Edge on a PC) and *Board shows a code*. Tapping a speaker opens its page in sections: **Sound** (volume, microphone), **Room mode** ("Listen now" as a Start/Stop button, duration, how much it may say, *What it may offer* with the same seven kinds as in the browser, stored per speaker on the Spark, room in Home Assistant, detect more, and with "Ignore TV voices" also who it listens to and trial), **Voice** (teach "My voice through this speaker"), **Firmware** (version, update now, updates by themselves, rewrite the firmware over USB), **Check** (checks with a readable board name, check network, sound and microphone test, live recent events, board log over USB) and *Remove speaker* at the bottom. Admin: under Features → Own speakers the firmware state comes first, then the address with *Check address* (tried from the Spark like "Check network", admin only, at most 6 checks in 10 minutes). Under People and devices and Me → Security speaker keys are marked "Speaker, managed under Me → Speakers"; they cannot be moved to another profile.

**Tasks and shopping list (V01.0.96):** "Put milk on the shopping list", "What is on my to-do list?". Admin switch, off at first, then for every profile. Each of the two lists lives either on the Spark, where an iPhone shortcut with the device key fetches new entries (`POST /api/tasks/inbox?list=einkauf`, header `X-Speech-Device`; a GET only reads and changes nothing) into the Reminders app, or in a CalDAV task list of a calendar account (Nextcloud, mailbox.org, Radicale). Adding happens at once, ticking off and deleting by voice only after "Yes". After outside text the assistant adds nothing in the same answer.

**Bus and train (V01.0.102):** "When does the next bus leave?", "How do I get to Karlsruhe at 8?". Admin switch, off at first, then for every profile. Timetable from transport.rest (default https://v6.db.transport.rest, own address configurable); fixed sentences with delay, platform and cancellations. With a commute (destination, time, days) "Proactive" speaks up 45 to 5 minutes before when the connection is 5 or more minutes late or cancelled. Timetable text counts as outside text.

**Reddit and feeds (V01.0.739):** "What's new on Reddit?", "Did anybody reply to me on Reddit?", "What's new in my feeds?". Admin switch Features → Reddit and feeds, off at first, then for every profile, never for guests. Each profile connects its own Reddit inbox under Me → Reddit and feeds with the private RSS link (reddit.com → Settings → RSS feeds; since November 2025 Reddit hands out API apps only on request, the link needs none), adds subreddits (r/LocalLLaMA) and other RSS or Atom feeds (blogs, YouTube channels, Mastodon) and enters up to 20 keywords. The link carries Reddit's feed key: connecting asks for the code (level "Confirm"), it is kept sealed in the vault and never sent back. The panel fetches each source at most every 30 minutes through the guard (https only, public addresses, 2 MB, after a 429 only after two hours), reads XML only with defusedxml (no entities, no DTD) and decides with fixed rules: what the first fetch finds was already there; new in the inbox means "a reply to you" and comes at once through "Proactive" ("News from feeds", preference on), a keyword as a whole word in a title at most 3 times a day, everything else goes into the daily briefing (up to 3 replies and 5 posts of the last 24 hours, keyword hits first, then the feed's order). Titles, senders and links are outside text: the tool `feeds_news` hands them over as data, after it switching, memory and proposals are locked; the page shows links only with https and `noopener`. The Spark never posts, votes or sends anything to Reddit.

**Agent functions (V01.0.175):** the assistant works longer for you. Admin: Features → "Agent functions" on and pick a level per profile: *off* (default), *read and report only* or *also act after "Yes"*. With the level the profile can start at once, Me → Jobs shows its jobs. Never guests, other voices or ESP32 speakers.
- *Background jobs:* "Research which heat pump fits the pool and let me know." The job runs in the background (search, read pages, with "my data" also appointments, reminders and documents), then a notification comes by browser, iPhone app and Telegram (as set under Me → Notifications). The full report is under Me → Jobs and in the log, on request as a document under "My documents" (switch "Also keep every report under "Documents"" or "As document" at the job). Limits: one job at a time on the Spark, it waits while someone talks to the assistant; 8 rounds, 4 searches, 5 pages, 12 tool calls, 15 minutes, 20,000 characters; 3 waiting and 20 jobs per day and profile. A job only has reading tools, its report counts as outside text and is never learned.
- *Scheduled jobs:* "Sum up my appointments of the week every Monday at 7." The assistant reads the plan back in fixed words, it is saved only after your "Yes". Once, daily, weekdays, weekly or monthly, at most 10, pause and delete under Me → Jobs. If the Spark was off for more than 3 hours at the planned time, that run is skipped.
- *Watching and own tasks (V01.0.508):* "Let me know when there is new firmware for my FRITZ!Box." or "Tell me as soon as the flight to Rome costs less than 100 euros." The panel recognizes the sentence itself, reads the watch out in fixed words (name, how often, price limit) and only makes it after your "Yes"; daily at 8 by default. Each run is a reading job; the model only names candidates (`FUND: value | address | quote`). **New conversation every day** (on by default, conversation settings → Display): the first question of a new day starts a new conversation; earlier ones stay in the history and can be picked and continued.

**Speaker identification** (off by default, Features): each profile reads three short sentences in the "Me" window. When the assistant then clearly recognizes a voice, it answers for that profile, with its memory, documents, reminders and voice, even on a guest device. The conversation shows "🎙 name" below the question. When someone other than the logged-in profile speaks at a device, the assistant gets only that one question (not the device's conversation), and the exchange stays out of the logged-in profile's history and memory. When the voice is unclear, nothing changes. Recognition runs on the Spark's CPU (Resemblyzer model, Apache 2.0) and needs no GPU memory. Strictness is adjustable. A voice is not a password: someone who sounds very similar or plays a recording can get into a profile.

**Profiles and memory:** under People and devices the admin creates profiles with a name and PIN. In the chat you log in with the "Guest" button at the top of the conversation by typing name and PIN. Logged in, the assistant remembers things for good when you tell it to ("remember that I'm vegetarian") or when they will be useful later, and forgets them on request; the Me lists what it knows, for deleting. Memory and conversations belong to the profile and live on the Spark, so the same history shows on every device you are logged in on: which profile is asking is decided only by the login (cookie) or the device key, never by the model, and guests get no memory. Speakers and your own programs get a device key under People and devices that belongs to one profile, and send it as the header `X-Speech-Device` to `/api/chat`. A new PIN logs out every browser of that profile; deleting a profile removes its memory and devices. The data lives on the Spark under `/var/lib/speech-spark/users`, one folder per profile.

**Home Assistant** (Funktionen → "Home Assistant", off by default): each profile connects its own Home Assistant in the "Me" window under "Smart home" with the address (e.g. `http://homeassistant.local:8123`) and a long-lived access token (Home Assistant: your profile → Security; best for a separate non-admin user). Then "turn on the living room light" or "how warm is it in the bathroom?" work in the conversation. Commands go to Home Assistant's own Assist (`/api/conversation/process`), so only entities exposed to Assist (Settings → Voice assistants → Expose) can be switched. Devices Assist does not know are switched directly through their service (e.g. `switch.turn_on`), except locks, alarm systems and updates. With a **code word** ("Me" window → Smart home) the assistant switches only when the code word is in the same message ("lights off, code word sunflower"); without it, it asks. The panel checks this itself, not the language model; the model and the stored conversations see only "[Codewort]", it is stored encrypted and compared with some tolerance for speech recognition (a letter or two off, "Apollo 13" = "Apollo dreizehn"). Questions need no code word. Also (ideas from ha-mcp): history ("how warm was it in the bathroom yesterday?"), several devices at once ("all lights in the living room off", each checked; an unknown room never switches the whole home), typo-tolerant device search and to-do lists such as the shopping list (read, add, tick off; changes need the code word). Questions about values, states, zones and where someone is are read directly (`/api/states`) instead: the assistant sees every device, sensor, zone and person the token's user may see, exposed or not. Home Assistant tells rooms (areas) only to an admin token; without admin rights devices are found by their names only. Only that profile can use its Home Assistant: guests never, and a voice recognized by speaker identification at someone else's device neither. The token is checked before saving, stays on the Spark (`users/<id>/homeassistant.json`, mode 0600) and is never sent back to the browser. When several devices fit a switching command, the device whose name, room or device class fits wins over one that only fits by its kind ("TV in the living room" is the TV there, not the Apple TV box next to it). If it stays unclear, the Spark switches nothing and names the fitting devices; the model then gets no switching tool (since V01.0.461).

**Ask instead of guessing** (Funktionen → "Rückfrage statt Raten", off by default; released for every profile): when several devices fit a switching command, the Spark switches nothing at random but asks: "Several fit: Samsung, Samsung The Frame or TV. Which one do you mean?". In the chat the devices are buttons below it, plus "None of these". When it cannot find the device exactly it asks "I can't find that exactly. Do you mean …?"; "None of these" or "show all" brings the whole device list with a search field, grouped by room (never locks, alarm panels or remotes). The answer may be typed, spoken or tapped: name, room or number ("the second"). It only counts in the same conversation, on the same device and for two minutes; the code word of the command covers the choice but is never stored or spoken. When the Spark itself picked one of several devices, "No, the Apple TV" corrects it: the wrongly switched device goes back to its old state, the right one is switched. Fixed rules in the panel decide, never the language model. (since V01.0.470)

**Remember the choice** (sub-switch, off by default): after a choice or a correction the Spark asks "Shall I remember that "TV in the living room" is Samsung?" (buttons "Yes, remember" and "No"). Only a yes stores it. From then on the same sentence switches that device at once; the code word is still needed. All mappings are under Me → Memory → "Mappings": change words, other target, delete, or assign your own from the device list with "Give own names". They live in the profile's `zuordnungen.json` and go with the memory into your own storage and the backup. The live page shows numbers only (open questions, asked today, chosen, corrected, remembered, used), the log fixed words only ("klaeren: frage ha 3 kandidaten"), never devices or sentences. (since V01.0.470)

**Asking back for bus and train, weather and messages too** (same switch): "to the station" fits several stops and names none exactly, so the Spark asks with buttons instead of taking the first; the same for place names in different regions ("Karlsbad" in Germany and Czechia) in the weather and for lists in Home Assistant ("Einkauf" fits "Einkaufsliste" and "Einkauf Baumarkt"; for a change the code word of the request covers the choice). The choice runs the same request with the chosen target; after a yes the word stands for it from then on (Me → Mappings, areas "Bus and train", "Places (weather)" and "Lists"). With "Do you mean Quirin Meier or Quirin Müller?" the names are buttons below. In Telegram the choices come as a keyboard under the answer; a tap sends the name like a typed answer. In the apps and at a speaker the spoken or typed answer does it (name, room or number). A calendar named for an appointment that does not exist is never silently replaced by the first one any more: the Spark enters nothing and names the calendars there are. (since V01.0.470)

**Searching conversations**: the magnifier next to the conversation list (on phones in the menu) searches all earlier conversations, a profile's with a profile, the browser's as a guest. A click opens the conversation at that spot.

**Earlier conversations** (Funktionen → "Frühere Gespräche", on by default; profiles only): the assistant can look up what you talked about before ("what did you tell me about the grill last week?", "what did we talk about yesterday?"), searching only your own profile's saved conversations. Conversations that have been quiet for ten minutes are also read once in the background, while nobody is talking, and up to three lasting facts per conversation go into the profile's memory, marked "automatic" in the "Me" window, where they can be deleted. Each profile can switch this learning off in the conversation settings ("Learn from conversations"). Once a week (or with "Propose cleanup") the assistant proposes merging duplicate entries and removing outdated ones; nothing changes until you accept the proposal under Me → Memory. With notifications on, a note is sent.

**Pronunciation:** Settings → Speech output → Pronunciation takes one rule per line, e.g. `DGX = De Ge Ix`. It applies to whole words and to all speech output, Open WebUI included.

**Barge-in:** while the assistant speaks, the microphone keeps listening. Talking for about a quarter of a second interrupts the answer, and what you said becomes the next question. This relies on the browser's echo cancellation; if the assistant interrupts itself on loud speakers, use headphones or untick "barge in" below the chat.

**Read answers aloud (since V01.0.409):** if you do not want speech output, switch it off. For one device, the "Vorlesen" switch below the face (on a phone the speaker icon at the top right of the chat) is enough; it applies to this browser or the Android app only. For all devices of the profile: conversation settings → answer → "Read answers aloud". The Spark then answers as text only, does not call the speech output at all (saves the GPU) and plays no instant acknowledgement either; notes, messages and reminders come with a chime and text but are not read aloud. Speakers without a screen and CarPlay keep speaking, there is nothing to read there. "Read again" on an answer still works. The Spark-Begleiter does not read "Frag Spark" answers aloud either while "Read answers aloud" is off.

**Your own voice:** under Voices you can record a reference straight from the microphone (read the suggested text, about 10 seconds); the panel fills in the transcript with the speech recognition. Cloning needs a Base model (Settings → Speech output → model `…-Base`), then pick the new voice as the default voice. The default voice is a list that fits the chosen model: CustomVoice shows the built-in speakers, Base your own voices, with VoiceDesign it is locked (the voice comes from the speaking style); a saved name missing from the list stays as "(not in the list)". A German reference gives much steadier German than the built-in speakers, which are not native German speakers. Under Voices, one or all of your own voices can be exported as a ZIP (reference audio and transcript) and imported again on this or another Spark; on a name clash the import renames, replaces or skips.

**Required tools and answer check (V01.0.117):** questions about appointments, mail, reminders, timers, weather, parcels, transit, lists, match results and news have to go through the matching tool (one table `NEED_TOOLS` in `chat.py`, also used by the quality test). While the model chooses the tool, the "tool choice" temperature (0.1) applies, the normal one afterwards. The answer check (Settings → Language model, on by default) lets no sentence through for such questions that names a time, date, score or amount that is in no tool result, the question or the conversation so far; instead the assistant says once "Genauere Angaben dazu habe ich nicht." If the model answers without looking anything up, the answer is dropped and the model is asked once more. Both show up in the tool log as "Antwort-Prüfung". General questions without a tool are not checked. Optional: the admin allows "think while choosing tools" (off); then it works for every profile, never for guests. Since V01.0.404 a round with thinking holds its text until the thinking is over: when the server does not hand the thinking over apart (reasoning_content) but as text up to a lone `</think>` (Qwen gets the opening `<think>` in the prompt), everything before it is never shown, spoken or kept; a lone `</think>` is never shown (before, the answer came twice with `</think>` between). Since V01.0.455 no sentence of an answer comes twice: when the model writes the answer before a tool call (also one it only writes as text) and again after the result, the next round shows each sentence only once it is complete and leaves out sentences already said, in the chat and in the voice; Logs → Requests then show "Repetition left out" with the count.

**Check deeds and stop error loops (V01.0.477):** two protection rules, on by default like the answer check (Settings → Language model, `chat.action_check`, `chat.loop_stop`, reason in `features.DEFAULT_ON`), without a switch per profile (`taten.py`, fixed rules, no model). **Check deeds:** every tool that changes something has a kind (`taten.KIND`: geraet, erinnerung, termin, mail_entwurf, dokument, remarkable, nachricht, merken, aufgabe, musik and more). Per answer the panel keeps which kind worked (the same failure words as Logs → Requests; "NOT saved yet" counts as a proposal, not as a deed). When a finished sentence claims a deed ("is on now", "I have entered it", "has been sent", "I'll remember", a bare "Done." while something that changes things was offered) and none of the kinds it names worked, it is neither shown nor spoken. At the end comes one fixed sentence: "That did not work: TV." (the tool ran and failed) or "I did not do that." (it did not run); nothing while a proposal is open. No second try. When a tool of that kind works later in the same answer ("I'm making the song." before `musik_machen`), the held sentence is left out silently (since V01.0.700). In doubt the sentence is said (it names several deeds and one of them worked). **Stop error loops:** when the same tool fails twice with the same details (or three times with any), it is not offered again for the rest of the answer, the model hears why, a further call is not run. Visible in Logs → Requests ("Taten-Prüfung", "Schleife gestoppt"), in the journal (`chat: action check: held back kind=… reason=…`, `chat: loop stop: tool=… fails=…`) and on the Live page ("Taten-Prüfung": held back and stopped today, numbers only). The settings assistant's rule set will use the same interface `taten.check(text, acts, offered)` (a fixed "built-in" rule).

**Three red questions in the quality test (V01.0.574):** (1) **Announced deed:** a sentence that only announces a tool call or a deed ("I'm calling the timer tool …", "I'll set a timer …", `taten.announces`) is neither shown nor spoken while no tool has worked in this answer. When a tool call follows, it is left out silently; when none follows, the panel says "I did not do that." at the end. Check deeds now also applies when no tool at all was offered (then every claimed deed is false). Journal: `chat: action check: held back kind=ankuendigung …`, `chat: action check: announcement left out, a tool ran`. (2) **Remember only what there is to remember:** a message that only asks (one sentence with a question mark, no "remember", nothing the person says about themselves like "I am …"; `chat.just_asks`) is not offered `memory_save` (`chat.offer_memory`, in the quality test as well), so "Do you know my brother's name?" never saves a made-up fact; journal `chat: memory_save not offered - the message only asks`. (3) **Account balance:** there is no bank tool, so every honest answer is right ("no access", "no tool", "check your banking app"). Since the fix after V01.0.597 the quality test checks no wording here, only facts: an amount in figures or words (`quality.MONEY`) or an unsuitable tool fails. The two cases without calendar and mail access still accept every "no access" answer (`quality.NO_ACCESS`).

(Warum … eben/gerade …) explains the way of the last answer (switch, tools, first sound, errors; from the memory of the last 24 hours); "How do the answers get faster?" names the catalog's steps. - **Assistent** (assistant): voice chat with the language model (the own one behind the LLM-Tor or qwen38). Speak (or type); the answer comes back as text and streamed speech, sentence by sentence while the LLM is still writing. Hands-free mode listens again after each answer; the space bar or "Speak" interrupts. If nobody says anything for 8 seconds after an answer, the microphone goes off and the status line says so ("Heard nothing, microphone off"); only "Hey Spark" listens all the time. **Follow-up without wake word** (admin: Features → Follow-up without wake word, off by default; profile: Me → Conversation, off/4/6/8/10 seconds): after answering a spoken question (via "Hey Spark" or "Speak") the microphone stays open for the chosen time, so "And tomorrow?" works without the wake word; if nothing comes it goes off and "Hey Spark" keeps listening. Unlike hands-free only briefly and only after spoken questions; never after typed questions, never for guests, never in room mode. The ESP32 speakers do not have it (they listen for "Jarvis" on the chip). On an iPhone, iOS ends the microphone when the screen locks, the page goes to the background, a call comes in or Siri speaks. The panel notices, says so, gets a fresh microphone the next time it listens and, in hands-free mode, listens again by itself once the page is visible. While iOS only holds the microphone (muted), that time does not count as silence.
- **Silence never becomes words** (fixed rule without a switch, `stille.py`, since the report of 2026-10-13): speech recognition models write something even when nobody speaks. After an answer the iPhone app listened again hands-free, a click, its own chime or the room came back as "Ja" or "Okay" and went to the assistant as a question. Now the panel measures for every recording at `/api/test/asr` (app, browser, guests) how long it is clearly louder than the quietest part of the room, in 30 ms pieces (three times the 20th percentile, at least about −46 dBFS, at most −30 dBFS). Under 0.1 s of voice the recording is silence, whatever was recognized. Sentences models write for silence ("Untertitel im Auftrag des ZDF", "Vielen Dank fürs Zuschauen", "Thanks for watching", "[Musik]") never count. A lone short word ("Ja", "Okay", "Danke", "Hm") counts only with at least 0.2 s of voice; a spoken "Ja" is longer, so a real yes still goes through. The answer is then empty with a reason (`silence`, `silence phrase`, `too short`); the browser shows "Heard nothing.", the log only `chat: ignored (silence)`, the Live overview "Stille überhört", never the text. Nothing of the sound is kept. The iPhone app (from the next TestFlight build) no longer records its chime and the 0.25 s from its start, and sends a recording only once it held at least 0.15 s of voice; when a hands-free recording comes back empty, the microphone goes off quietly ("Nichts gehört, Mikrofon aus.").
- **TV and other voices never become a question** (fixed rule without a switch, `fremdstimme.py`, since the report of 2026-10-13, request d80a75): after an answer the browser opens the microphone by itself: for barge-in while the answer plays, for the follow-up without wake word and in hands-free mode. With the TV on, its voices went to the assistant as a new question and cut that answer off again. Now the page says for every such recording how the microphone opened (`opened` = `barge`, `follow` or `hands`); a tapped "Speak" and "Hey Spark" are never checked. (1) When the profile taught its voice and speaker recognition is on, the panel compares the recording with the voiceprint (the profile's strictness): it counts when it is the profile's voice or clearly another household profile's, and also when it is too short to compare ("And tomorrow?"), so no real follow-up is lost.
