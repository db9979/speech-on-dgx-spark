# iPhone app "Spark"

An own app for the iPhone (source in `ios/`): press the button, ask, the answer comes in the Spark voice, also with the screen locked. "Hey Siri, Ask Spark" (German phrase "Frag Spark") runs through the app without a shortcut, also with AirPods and through Siri in the car. Since V01.0.676 the app has its own CarPlay icon in the car (Apple granted the entitlement).

## Switch it on in the panel

1. Admin: **Features → iPhone app** on, save.
2. This works at once for every profile. Only the admin blocks single profiles (**People and devices → Blocked functions**).
3. To switch lights and devices from the app: **"Smart home from the app"** on. The code word applies as everywhere.
4. For the wake word and stand mode: **"Allow listening all the time"** on.
5. To start routes and calls by voice: **"Routes and calls on the iPhone"** on.
6. Notes on its own (morning briefing, reminders) come from the start; they can be switched off under **Me → By itself**. Reminders and timers ring as iPhone notifications, also with the app closed.

## Getting the app onto the iPhone (on a Mac, about 15 minutes once)

1. Install **Xcode** from the Mac App Store (free) and open it once.
2. Xcode → **Settings → Accounts → +** → sign in with your Apple ID. A free account is enough to try it; the app then runs for 7 days and has to be installed again. With the Apple Developer Program (99 €/year) it runs for a year and works with TestFlight.
3. Get the repo: `git clone https://github.com/db9979/speech-on-dgx-spark.git` (or `git pull`).
4. Double-click `ios/Spark.xcodeproj`.
5. Click the project **Spark** → target **Spark** → **Signing & Capabilities** → pick your account as **Team**. Do the same for the second target **SparkNotify** (it fetches the text of push notifications). If Xcode says the bundle ID is taken: project **Spark** (not the target) → **Build Settings** → search `APP_BUNDLE_ID` and add something of your own; both targets take it over.
6. Connect the iPhone by cable, unlock it, confirm "Trust This Computer".
7. On the iPhone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode** on (the iPhone restarts).
8. In Xcode pick your iPhone as the destination at the top, then press **▶︎ (Run)**.
9. On first start the iPhone says "Untrusted Developer": **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust**. Then open the app.

## Pairing

1. In the panel **Me → iPhone app → "Pair an iPhone"**.
2. On a PC: scan the QR code with the iPhone camera. On the iPhone: tap **"open in the app"**.
3. The app asks "Pair with this Spark?" and shows the address. Tap **Pair** only for your own address.

The link is valid for 10 minutes and once. The address must be https with a real certificate (your reverse proxy); the self-signed certificate on port 31443 is not accepted. If you open the panel for pairing through port 31443 or an IP address, the panel says so right under the QR code. If the iPhone later does not accept the certificate, the app names the address and the reverse proxy in the "Spark not reachable" note: after the panel moves (e.g. to the Raspberry Pi) the reverse proxy must point to the new computer; the app keeps its address and its key.

**With an invitation** (since V01.0.276): when someone without a profile opens the invitation link on the iPhone and taps "Rather straight in the iPhone app", the app creates the profile with name and PIN and is paired right away. The app shows the Spark's address first. An app that is already paired takes no invitation (unpair first); PIN links work only in the browser.

**With a review code** (since V01.0.754, for Apple's App Store review): the admin switches on **Features → Review access for the iPhone app**, picks a profile under **People and devices → Review access for the iPhone app** (best one of its own such as "App Review", never one with an admin role) and taps "Make a review access". The panel shows the https address and a code of twelve letters and digits (`ABCD-EFGH-JKMN`, without 0/O and 1/I/L) once; only its hash is stored. In the app type both under **Connect with a review code** (case, spaces and dashes do not matter); the app asks "Pair with this Spark?" as with a link. The code is valid 14 days for at most 5 iPhones, at most 3 review accesses at a time. Wrong codes count like wrong PINs: after several the address is locked for a while. While a profile has a review access, smart home, mail, calendars, contacts, calls, chats, Telegram, jobs, MCP, speakers, room mode, announcements, phone, companion, Spark update, reports, NAS, storage and reMarkable are blocked for it, whatever else is on. Deleting the review access removes the iPhones paired with it at once; after 14 days this happens by itself.

**Look without a Spark** (since V01.0.754): at the bottom of the pairing screen the app shows a demo with examples, without pairing and without network. The app answers typed questions itself with fixed examples; speaking does not work there (speech recognition runs on the Spark). **End the demo** at the top left goes back to pairing.

## Using it

- The assistant's face at the top is the same as in the panel (robot, comic or comic woman; since V01.0.521 you pick it yourself at the top of Settings → *Face* in the app or in the panel under Me → Conversation, else the admin's default applies; the app takes it over when it opens and after the pick, with the next TestFlight build). It blinks, looks around, listens with a red antenna, thinks with a turning arc and moves its mouth while speaking. Tapping it works like the button. With "Face shows what it does" on in the panel (V01.0.323), the app's face shows the same as the panel: a magnifier while searching, a calendar sheet, a letter, a light bulb for the smart home, a note pad when remembering, think bubbles, a tick with a wink after something was done, a question mark on an error and "zzz" after 5 quiet minutes. Driven only by the Spark's events, never by the answer's text.
- Big button: tap, speak; the app notices when you are done (or tap again). Tapping while the Spark speaks stops it.
- Questions can also be typed. Top left starts a new conversation; after 10 minutes of quiet it starts anew by itself.
- "Hey Siri, Frag Spark" (or "Hey Siri, Spark fragen"): Siri asks for the question and reads the answer aloud.

In the app under **Settings (gear)**:

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hands-free | After each answer the app listens again (its chime and clicks never count as a question, see "Silence never becomes words" in assistant.md). 8 seconds of silence: microphone off. Since V01.0.272 a value of your profile: browser and app show the same (like "Interrupt"). |
| Interrupt | Just start talking to stop the answer and be heard. The iPhone filters the Spark's own voice out of the microphone. With "Barge-in: pause first" (admin, Features → Conversation) the answer only pauses: a "mhm" lets it play on, "stop" ends it, a sentence becomes the new question; without a decision after 4 seconds it plays on (see assistant.md). |
| Read answers aloud | Off: answers as text only. The switch belongs to the profile (like in the browser, all devices). "Read aloud on this iPhone" applies to this iPhone only. In CarPlay the app always speaks (since V01.0.409). |
| Wake word | "Hey Spark", "Jarvis" or "Computer". The iPhone recognizes it itself, offline and without Apple's servers; only then does anything go to your Spark. If the iPhone cannot do German offline, it stays off. |
| On battery | How long the wake word listens on battery after the last use (charger only, 30 minutes, 1 or 3 hours). Always on the charger. Default since V01.0.668: charger only (battery). |
| Stand mode | On the charger: big face, clock, last answer, screen stays on, dimmed at night. |
| Speak on its own | The app says the Spark's notes aloud while it is open. |

Routes and calls: "Navigiere zur Arbeit" or "Ruf Anna an". The app asks every time ("Open route to …?", "Call Anna (number)?"). Only "Ja" (tapped or said) opens Maps or calls. Contacts are searched only on the iPhone; the number does not go to the Spark.

## Quick start, history, photos, offline

- **Quick start:** "Talk to Spark" opens the app and it listens at once. On the Action button: iPhone Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → "Talk to Spark". In Control Center (iOS 18 and later) via "Add a Control", on the lock screen as a widget. With Siri: "Hey Siri, Start Spark".
- **History:** The clock icon at the top left shows your earlier conversations, the same list as the panel's log. Tapping one continues it. Deleting stays in the panel.
- **Today** (since V01.0.286): the sun at the top right shows what is due today, like *Me → Overview* in the panel: next appointments of today and tomorrow, open reminders, unread messages, documents, remembered facts. Only cards of functions your profile may use; pull down to refresh.
- **Ask about a photo or document:** The paper clip next to the input: take a photo, choose a photo, or a document (PDF, text, image). The iPhone reads the text itself, scanned pages too. Only the text goes to the Spark. The attachment stays for the conversation until you remove it with ✕. **Let the Spark look at photos:** with "Let the model look at photos from the app" on under Me → iPhone app (plus the admin switch for pictures), a photo goes to the Spark as a picture, scaled to 1280 px, so it also recognises things, plants or an error display. Follow-up questions about the same photo work for 10 minutes. With it off, the iPhone reads only the text as before. PDFs and documents always stay text.
- **Save to "My things":** With own documents released for your profile, the attachment shows a button for it. The Spark then finds the document later, like an upload in the panel. Since V01.0.723 there is no profile switch for it any more.
- **Offline:** When the Spark cannot be reached, the app says so at the top. Typed questions wait (at most 10, for one day) and go out once the Spark is back. Speaking does not work then, because the Spark recognises the speech.
- **Calm, nothing jumps** (since V01.0.570): the note "Spark not reachable" comes only after 3 seconds without the Spark, so a short network change or the restart during an update does not make it blink. When messages arrive from another device, the app only appends the new ones; the history is not drawn anew. An open page under Settings stays open even when the admin blocks it right then (with a note on top; Back closes it). Monitoring and Checks show an error only on the second failed try and at the end of the list, rows in Logs and hints keep their place. With the charger, the history stays in place behind the stand view.
- **Last state at once** (since V01.0.748, function "Apps keep the last state"): history, chats, My profile, settings and "Open in the panel" show the state of last time as soon as they open, with "As of 16:05 · refreshing …" on top until the Spark has answered (then the note goes, or it says "Spark not reachable right now"). Kept are the 20 newest conversations with 50 messages each, the chat list and the last page of up to 20 chats, the own settings and functions and the numbers on the tiles of "My things" (only how many, no name); private chats and chats with "messages vanish" never. Without a kept state the page shows grey placeholders, after a second "Connecting to the Spark …", after 8 seconds "This takes longer than usual …" and on an error "Try again". When the app comes to the front it fetches history, chats and settings once in the background (at most every 5 minutes). When a page needs more than 2 seconds, Status → Logs (area Apps) shows a line `app: langsam <page> <ms> ms iphone`.
- **Save the battery** (since V01.0.668): in the background the app stops its audio engine as soon as nothing listens, speaks or calls any more; an answer still playing is finished first. iOS can then put the app to sleep. Exception: the wake word listens (on the charger, or for the time under "On battery"). In the car the app lets go of the sound after every conversation since V01.0.871, also in front, so radio and music do not stay quieter.
- **English:** On an iPhone set to English the app is in English, including the wake word and Siri ("Ask Spark", "Start Spark").
- **Long questions** (since V01.0.829): the input field grows up to five lines, beyond that it scrolls. The return key makes a new line, the arrow sends. From four lines ⤢ at the top right of the field opens the large view over the whole screen: *Done* puts the text back into the field, *Send* sends it.

## My profile

In the app under Settings → **My profile** you set the same values as in the panel; changes apply on both sides at once:

- **Voice:** voice and speaking speed, with "Listen to a sample".
- **Answers:** answer length. Your wishes for the tone are only shown; they change in the panel, because the language model is told them.
- **By itself:** on/off, quiet time, at most so many notes a day, appointments with lead time, weather with place and time, parcels, birthdays, bus and train, greeting, mails, morning briefing with time. Shown only when the admin has "By itself" on. Topics whose service is off in the profile are greyed out.
- **What the app may do:** shown only. Change it in the panel under Me → iPhone app.

## Reminders, sharing, Shortcuts

- **Apple Reminders:** With "Apple Reminders on the iPhone" (Me → iPhone app, off) the app asks after each Spark reminder "… also put into the Reminders app?". After "yes" it is in the list "Spark", without its own alarm (the Spark rings already). When the Spark cancels a reminder it goes there too. New shopping and to-do entries come into the lists "Shopping" and "To-dos" when the app opens (each entry once). A list kept in a CalDAV account stays there.
- **Share → Spark:** In Safari, Mail, Files or Photos tap Share → Spark. Text, web page (the iPhone loads it itself), PDF or photo: the iPhone reads the text (a photo goes as a picture with the photo switch on), then you ask about it or store it under "My documents" (with the documents switch). The answer can be shared, e.g. as a note.
- **Shortcuts:** The Shortcuts app gets "Ask Spark", "Reminder at the Spark", "Add to a Spark list" and "Store a document at the Spark". Lists need the reminders switch, documents the documents switch.
- **Notes:** Apple does not let apps write into Notes directly. Two ways: long-press an answer in the app → "Share (e.g. as a note)", or a shortcut of "Ask Spark" plus Apple's "Create Note".

## Panel areas in the app

The app takes over the panel bit by bit (plan "iPhone app takes over the panel"). The admin switches on "Panel areas in the iPhone app" under Features; that applies at once to every profile with a paired iPhone, only the admin blocks single profiles (People and devices → Blocked functions). The former switches per area under Me → iPhone app are gone since the app parity (Dominik 2026-10-14). Everything starts off, guests get none of it. The app itself cannot switch an area on.

- **Open in the panel** (Settings): whatever the app does not show itself opens the panel right here in the app at the right place (the first time you sign in as in the browser). The list shows every page of Me your profile can use now, reMarkable, storage, writing help, companion and MCP too; it comes from the Spark (`/api/features`, list `pages` from `features.ME_PAGES`), so a new page shows up without an app update. Only overview, let's go, sign in and Android app are left out, with a reason (`features.APP_SKIP`). With "Manage the Spark" also monitoring, logs, features and profiles.
- **Features and People and devices under Manage the Spark** (since V01.0.276): the names and groups of the switches come from the Spark, the app keeps no list of its own. A profile also shows its functions "n of m on" to switch, its admin role and the signed-in browsers (swipe to sign out).
- **My day**: the next appointments of your calendars and the Spark's reminders (swipe to delete). Under My profile the conversation switches join: learn from conversations, a new conversation every day and, when the admin allows them, learn from corrections, think when choosing tools and targeted tool choice. Since V01.0.723 the memory is under My things.
- **Manage the Spark** (only for profiles with the Admin or Manager role): after Face ID the app counts the way your profile is signed in, without an admin sign-in of its own (since V01.0.541). The app key only counts if it was created after your second login step was switched on or you confirmed that step once in this app since; the panel password works only in the browser. When the app is not admin right now, "Spark verwalten" says why (since V01.1.266, fixed words from the Spark): app paired before the (newly set up) second step, no second step, no role, app blocked or your own emergency stop. In the first case **Confirm with code** (code or passkey, asked every time) binds this app's key to the second step as it is now; if you set the step up again, the app asks again. Inside: **Summary** ("Needs you", memory, CPU/GPU, services), **Logs** (diagnosis as in the panel with areas and time span, "Copy for thread" and share), **Checks** (function check, quality test, priority check) and **Workshop** (since V01.0.886, admin only: the workshop's results, newest first, each with what it means and the next step; looking into a cause and repairing open the panel's page Status → Workshop in the app). Under **Change**: **Remote access** (since V01.1.213, admin only: the paired remote servers with their state, Disconnect, Disconnect all, May connect and Remove, only after "Unlock (code/passkey)" in the app as on the panel page (since V01.1.254), with "Extend" and "Lock"; a system from the Windows file with "Confirm (code/passkey)" (since V01.1.277); shell and pairing open the panel's page Remote access → Systems in the app), **Features** (the plain on/off switches; sensitive ones like "Port", the second login step for profiles and the iPhone switches themselves stay in the browser), **People and devices** (create a profile, remove devices, new PIN, reset the second step, delete a profile; a code only at "change protection") and **Backups** (back up now; deleting old ones only as admin; restoring and downloading in the browser). A manager sees only the summary, logs and people and devices; there they lift a profile's blocks but switch no function and block nothing (only the admin does, since V01.0.551).
- **Teach your voice**: the app shows three sentences, you read them aloud, saving needs the code from the authenticator app. Below, "Teach" for each of your speakers (the speaker then asks for five sentences). Needs speaker recognition from the admin and your profile's second login step.
- **Proactive**: weather place, parcels from e-mails, bus and train (search and set a stop), look at and stop your jobs, room mode "+30 min" (at most four hours from now). The switches weather, parcels, bus and train and jobs are here too.
- **Security**: your devices (swipe to remove), recent logins, log out everywhere, turn the second step off or new recovery codes (with a code). Setting it up works only in the browser with the PIN: otherwise someone with your unlocked iPhone alone could set their own second step.
- **Connect accounts**: mail, calendar, contacts, smart home and Telegram. Only with your profile's second login step and a fresh code. The Spark checks the connection, stores password or token encrypted and never hands it out; the iPhone does not keep it.

## My things

Since V01.0.723 (Dominik 2026-10-17, plan `app-meine-sachen/plan.md`) everything the Spark keeps for you is on one page: the icon with four squares at the top left beside the conversation, or Settings → **My things**. Tiles with their numbers show Documents, Made by the Spark, Pictures, Music and Memory, below them "Recently added". The search at the top covers everything: titles, file names, senders, tags, picture wishes, songs and remembered facts (it runs on the iPhone, nothing goes to the Spark for it).

Each tile follows only the admin's release of its function (Own documents, Creating documents, Painting pictures, Making music, Memory), without a profile switch and without the switch "Panel areas in the iPhone app". At the bottom "Switches for documents" with the switches of Me → Documents, as far as the admin allows them, and "Left-out folders" (since V01.0.778): the list of folders and names the Spark never reads, with "Let in again", adding name rules (asking first how many documents leave the search) and removing them with a swipe; leaving out a single folder happens in the folder tree in the panel.

## Security

- Each iPhone gets its own key when pairing, kept only in that iPhone's keychain and on the Spark only as a hash.
- The key may only ask and listen (chat, speech recognition, Siri question). Settings, memory, devices, connections and further pairings are refused.
- When the admin blocks a profile (People and devices → Blocked functions), all its iPhones are locked at once, the admin switch locks all of them. Remove a lost iPhone under **Me → iPhone app → Remove**.
- Smart home from the app only with its own switch and the code word. What the app claims to be does not count; the panel decides by the key.
- Wake word, stand mode, routes and calls only with their own switch in the panel. The Spark only suggests a route or call; the tool exists only for the app key with the switch, and only your "yes" on the iPhone starts it. Outside text (mail, web page) cannot start a route or call.
- No sound goes to the Spark before the wake word. In the background the app listens only with the wake word on.
- Push: the Apple key (.p8) is stored encrypted, changed only by the admin with the second step, never shown again. Apple only gets a fixed sentence and a random number. Only the app with its own key signs up its push address; a removed iPhone gets nothing from then on. Only the profile's own app gets a notification's text.
- Photos and documents: the iPhone reads the text, only text goes to the Spark (at most 20,000 characters per question). Photos go as pictures only with the photo switch on; a round with a picture offers no tools, and the picture is not kept in the history. The Spark treats it as outside text: it sits as data in the prompt, not in the conversation, and locks actions as after a mail. The history keeps only the attachment's name.
- Storing documents only with its own switch, only with the app key, at most 10 per minute and 3 MB. The app cannot list or delete the documents.
- "My profile" changes only a fixed list (voice, speed, length, by itself, morning briefing). The app's rights, Telegram, the smart home and the tone cannot be changed through it; a request with any other field is refused as a whole.
- Apple Reminders: only with its own switch, only after "yes" on the iPhone. The app reads nothing from Reminders for the Spark. Shortcuts may only add entries to the lists; list settings stay in the panel.
- Sharing: shared content is outside text (locks actions), at most 20,000 characters per question; the iPhone loads web pages itself, at most 3 MB.
- Waiting questions stay on the iPhone and are deleted when unpairing.
- Last state (admin switch `chat.app_cache`, on in the basic set, blockable per profile): the app keeps only while `GET /api/iphone/hello` says "keep"; when the Spark says no it deletes everything at once. The files lie in the app's own storage with data protection "complete" (unreadable while locked), never in an iCloud or computer backup, in one folder per pairing (its name is a hash of address and key, never the key). Unpairing, a new pairing and another profile delete everything. Never kept: keys, codes, "Manage Spark", private and vanishing chats, files and pictures. Kept rights switch nothing on (microphone, push, calls, Apple Reminders wait for the Spark's answer).
- Spark update: only the admin gives the rights (with the admin's code when the second step is on); a profile cannot give them to itself. Starting needs the app's key, Face ID and a fresh 6-digit code of the profile (each code once, wrong codes count towards the lock), at most once in 10 minutes. The app cannot pick a version; only versions with passed GitHub tests are installed. Every start is in the security log, and the language model has no tool for it.
- CarPlay never loosens anything: in the car the smart home is locked without its own car switch.
- Calls: sound and picture travel encrypted (DTLS-SRTP) directly between the devices or through the Spark's own relay, never through Apple. When ringing, Apple only gets a random number; name and kind the app fetches from the Spark with its key. The app key reaches only the call paths (start, answer, signal, end, info, ICE), and only for calls its profile takes part in. The language model cannot start a call.
- Private chats: the iPhone's key lives in the Secure Enclave and never leaves it; the Spark only sees encrypted messages. A new key needs the second step's code, like a new device.
- Pairing only from the profile's own browser login, with the second login step when the profile has it. At most 5 iPhones per profile.
